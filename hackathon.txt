New Agent. Build a new Slack agent using one of the above three technologies to automate workflows, connect systems, or surface intelligent insights inside Slack and submit it to the Slack Marketplace before the hackathon deadline; or

Apps submitted to this track must follow all Slack Marketplace guidelines and requirements, including being installed in 5 active workspaces.
Apps submitted to the Slack Marketplace must be deployed in a production workspace, not a developer sandbox. Your developer sandbox is for testing and judging only.
If you don't have a production workspace, create a free Slack workspace to build and deploy your app, then use your developer sandbox for testing.
