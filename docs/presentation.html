02 / 07

The problem

Decisions are made in Slack.
Then they're lost in Slack.

A bug gets described in a thread — someone re-types it into Jira, guesses the priority, hunts down an owner. Three weeks later nobody remembers what was decided, or why. The knowledge evaporates. The busywork stays.

03 / 07

What Loop does

Remembers

Decisions and commitments are stored with provenance — who said it, where, and when — and recalled semantically, however you re-phrase the question. Even from channels nobody @mentioned it in.

Acts

Describe a bug and it stages a Jira ticket — priority, project, and the assignee resolved from a first name to a real account. A human says yes; the ticket exists. Proposes, never presumes. Filed live on stage today: KAN-22.

Shows its work

Every turn writes telemetry: tokens, tools, latency — and the model's own reasoning, hidden from the room but kept for audit. Observable. Debuggable. Improvable.

Why it's different

The only vendor lock-in
is an environment variable.

This demo runs on MiniMax M3 through an Anthropic-compatible endpoint. The same agent runs on Claude, GPT, Bedrock, Gemini, or a local Ollama model — change one line of .env. Self-hosted, one Python process, local SQLite, local embeddings. Your conversations never leave your infrastructure.
